# Magneetar Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2, 2026
**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

## Introduction

Magneetar ("we," "our," "us") is an anti-theft tracking application designed to help you protect your mobile device. We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable data protection laws.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Data We Collect

### Device Information (Required)
- **Device identifier** (unique app-generated ID)
- **Device model and OS version**
- **IMEI number** (encrypted, only for device identification and police reports)
- **SIM card serial number** (encrypted, for theft detection only)
- **Battery level and charging status**
- **Network type and signal strength**

### Location Data (Required for Core Function)
- **GPS coordinates** (latitude, longitude, accuracy)
- **Location timestamp**
- **Speed and bearing** (for movement detection)
- **WiFi access points** (for location accuracy improvement)

### Security Events (Automatic)
- **Failed unlock attempts** (for theft detection)
- **SIM card changes** (for theft detection)
- **Device admin status changes** (for theft detection)
- **App installation/uninstallation events**

### Optional Data (With Explicit Consent)
- **Photos** (captured only during suspected theft events)
- **Audio recordings** (captured only during suspected theft events)
- **Contacts** (for emergency contact features)

## How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Legal Basis (GDPR) | Retention Period |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| Device tracking and recovery | Consent | 90 days (configurable) |
| Theft detection and alerts | Legitimate interest | 90 days |
| Evidence capture (photos/audio) | Consent | Until case resolved |
| Service improvement | Legitimate interest | Anonymized after 90 days |
| Customer support | Contract | Until support resolved |

## Data Storage and Security

- **Encryption at rest:** All location data, IMEI, and sensitive information is encrypted using AES-256-GCM
- **Encryption in transit:** All communications use TLS 1.3 with certificate pinning
- **Data location:** Your data is stored on secure servers in the region closest to you
- **Access controls:** Only you can access your data. We cannot read your location data without your encryption key
- **Data isolation:** Your data is segregated from other users' data at the database level

## Data Sharing

**We do NOT sell your data.** We share your data only in these limited circumstances:

1. **With your explicit consent:** When you share your location with family members through Magneetar Circles
2. **Legal requirements:** When required by valid legal process (e.g., court order)
3. **Service providers:** Our infrastructure providers (hosting, database) who are contractually bound to protect your data

### Third-Party Services
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** Used for push notifications (Google's privacy policy applies)
- **Sentry:** Used for crash reporting (no personal data included)
- **Cloudflare:** Used for network security (standard CDN privacy)

## Your Rights (NDPR/GDPR)

You have the right to:

1. **Access** — Request a copy of all data we hold about you
2. **Rectification** — Request correction of inaccurate data
3. **Erasure** — Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
4. **Restriction** — Request限制 processing of your data
5. **Portability** — Receive your data in a machine-readable format
6. **Objection** — Object to processing based on legitimate interests
7. **Withdraw consent** — Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing)

### How to Exercise Your Rights
- **In-app:** Settings → Privacy → Export Data / Delete Account
- **Email:** privacy@magneetar.me
- **Response time:** Within 72 hours for most requests; 30 days for complex requests

## Consent and Control

### Location Sharing Consent
- Location sharing requires **explicit, informed consent** before activation
- You can revoke consent at any time through Settings → Privacy
- Revoking consent immediately stops all location tracking

### Evidence Capture Consent
- Photo and audio capture require **separate, explicit consent**
- These features are OFF by default and must be explicitly enabled
- You can disable these features at any time without affecting core tracking

### Family Circle Consent
- Joining a Circle requires your **explicit consent**
- You can leave any Circle at any time with one tap
- Circle members cannot track you without your ongoing consent

## Children's Privacy

Magneetar is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

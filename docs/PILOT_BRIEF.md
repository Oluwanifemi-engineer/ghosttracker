# Magneetar — Pilot Brief

## What is Magneetar?

Magneetar is an anti-theft tracking app for Android. If your phone is stolen, it keeps reporting its location, captures evidence (photos + audio), and lets you lock or alarm it remotely — even if the thief changes the SIM card.

**Not a replacement for Find My Device.** A supplement for when Find My Device fails: phone offline, SIM changed, Google account locked out, or budget phone without reliable GMS.

## What you get (free, during pilot)

- **Real-time tracking** — your phone reports location every 3 seconds
- **Theft detection** — Sentinel AI scores suspicious activity (SIM change, failed unlocks, device admin disabled) and auto-captures evidence
- **Remote commands** — lock, siren alarm, front-camera photo, audio recording, full wipe
- **Geofencing** — set safe zones, get alerted when your phone leaves
- **SMS commands** — when your phone is offline, commands arrive via SMS
- **Push alerts** — theft, SIM change, geofence exit → instant notification
- **Web dashboard** — see all your devices on a live map from any browser

## How to install

1. Download the APK from the link we send you
2. Enable "Install from unknown sources" (Settings → Security)
3. Open the APK and install
4. Open Magneetar → Sign Up → Use your real email
5. Follow the permission prompts (location, camera, microphone, notifications)
6. That's it — the app runs silently in the background

## What it does NOT do

- Does not drain your battery noticeably (location uses fused GPS, not raw GPS)
- Does not spy on you (photos are only captured when theft is detected)
- Does not share your location with anyone (only you can see it on the dashboard)
- Does not work on iPhones (Android only)

## Tips

- **Send Message 1 to professionals, Message 2 to friends/family**
- **Don't send to more than 5 people at once** — WhatsApp might flag bulk messages
- **Personalize every message** — add something specific to the person ("I know you travel a lot for work" or "Your kids commute to school every day")
- **Follow up within 24 hours** if they say yes — momentum matters
- **Don't beg** — if someone says no, move on. You need 10, not 100% conversion
